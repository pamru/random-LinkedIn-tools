# Etsy Listing Guide — Editable Mechanic Invoice PDF Template

This guide walks you through listing the editable mechanic invoice PDF
template on Etsy, from preparing files to publishing the listing.

## What's in this bundle

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `mechanic_invoice_template_BLANK.pdf` | The product file you deliver to buyers. All fields are blank and editable. |
| `mechanic_invoice_template_SAMPLE.pdf` | Sample filled with dummy data — use for listing thumbnails and screenshots. Do **not** include this in the download buyers receive. |
| `mechanic_invoice_template.py` | Source script (optional — not for sale). Regenerates both PDFs. |

## Step 1 — Prepare the downloadable product

1. Open `mechanic_invoice_template_BLANK.pdf` in Adobe Acrobat Reader
   (free) or Preview (Mac) and confirm every field is empty and clickable.
2. Rename the file to something buyer-friendly, e.g.
   `Editable-Mechanic-Invoice-Template.pdf`.
3. (Optional) Add a second copy with your own watermark removed plus a
   short `README.txt` explaining how to fill it in (see Step 6).
4. Zip the final deliverables into one archive:
   `Mechanic-Invoice-Template.zip` containing:
   - `Editable-Mechanic-Invoice-Template.pdf`
   - `How-to-Use.txt` (optional, recommended)

## Step 2 — Create listing thumbnails from the sample

Etsy allows up to 10 images per listing. Use the **filled** sample PDF
as the source.

1. Convert the sample PDF to high-resolution PNGs. On macOS Preview:
   *File → Export → Format: PNG → 300 dpi*. Or use any online PDF → PNG
   converter.
2. Crop to Etsy's recommended size: **2000 × 2000 px** (square) for
   thumbnails, **2700 × 2025 px** for horizontal hero shots.
3. Suggested image slots:
   1. Hero shot: the full invoice on a neutral background.
   2. Close-up of the top header (logo + invoice number).
   3. Close-up of the repair services table with line items.
   4. Close-up of totals + payment method section.
   5. Mockup: the PDF shown on a laptop or iPad (use Placeit or Canva).
   6. "What's included" infographic — list of features.
   7. "How to edit" screenshot showing Acrobat Reader in use.
   8. Before/after: blank template next to the filled sample.

## Step 3 — Create the Etsy listing

Sign in at https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me and click **Add a
listing**, then **Digital file**.

### Listing basics

- **Title** (140 chars max, keyword-rich):
  `Editable Mechanic Invoice Template PDF | Auto Repair Shop Invoice |
  Printable & Fillable Car Service Receipt | Instant Download`
- **About this listing:** *I made it / A finished product / Made to
  order: No*
- **Category:** Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Stationery → Invoice
- **Renewal options:** Automatic
- **Type:** Digital

### Description (sample copy you can adapt)

```

Keep your auto shop's paperwork clean and professional with this
fillable PDF invoice. Type directly into every field — no Photoshop,
Canva, or extra software required. Print, email, or save as PDF.
```

## Step 4 — Pricing & files

- **Price:** Typical range for editable invoice templates is
  **$4–$12 USD**. Competitive sweet spot: **$6.99**.
- **Quantity:** 999 (digital, so effectively unlimited).
- **Digital files:** Upload `Editable-Mechanic-Invoice-Template.pdf`
  (or the ZIP from Step 1). Etsy allows up to 5 files, 20 MB each.

## Step 5 — Preview & publish

1. Click **Preview** and check every field on the listing page.
2. Confirm the first thumbnail is sharp at small sizes.
3. Click **Publish**.
4. Pin to a board or share on Pinterest — invoice templates get strong
   traffic from Pinterest searches.

## Step 6 — (Optional) "How-to-Use.txt" for buyers

Include this text file in the download so buyers get clear instructions:

```
THANK YOU FOR YOUR PURCHASE!

HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE
1. Download and save the PDF to your computer.
2. Open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free):
   https://get.adobe.com/reader/
3. Click any highlighted field and start typing.
4. Use Tab to jump to the next field.
5. File → Save As to keep a copy per customer.
6. Print or email the finished invoice.

REPLACING THE LOGO
- Acrobat Pro: Tools → Edit PDF → Add Image.
- Free options: Sejda (sejda.com), PDFescape (pdfescape.com).
- Tip: delete the dashed placeholder box first, then drop your logo
  image in the same spot.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Fields not clickable? Make sure you're opening in Acrobat Reader,
  not a browser preview.
- Printing in black & white? Use Acrobat's "Print in grayscale" option.

Need help? Message me through Etsy — I'm happy to assist!
```

## Step 7 — After you publish

- Respond to buyer messages within 24 hours (Etsy ranks fast responders
  higher).
- Ask happy buyers to leave a review — reviews drive Etsy search rank.
- Refresh the listing every 2–3 months by tweaking tags and thumbnails
  to stay visible.
